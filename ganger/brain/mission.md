# desktop-keeping-reading

## In the foreman's words

I will build a small desktop program for your laptop that keeps a reading list of books. You can add a book with its title and author, and mark it as read. Everything stays on your computer, needs no account, and works without internet on Windows, Mac and Linux.

## What it is for

A desktop app for keeping a simple reading list of books

## Who uses it

me, on my laptop

## What it must do on day one

- add a book with its title and author
- mark a book as read

## Shape

- Accounts: none
- Connectivity: offline-first
- Stack: Desktop program, with no backend
